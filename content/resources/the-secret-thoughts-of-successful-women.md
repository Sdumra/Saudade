  For the graduate student who has convinced herself that everyone around her
  actually belongs here and she is the only one faking it.

  Valerie Young spent years studying imposter syndrome in academic and scientific spaces. This book names the exact patterns — the Perfectionist, the Expert, the Soloist — so you can recognize which one you are. Not generic advice. Specific, honest, and written for women who have earned their place and still don't believe it.


  Read this if you have ever attributed your success to luck, stayed quiet in a meeting, or waited to feel ready before speaking up.
